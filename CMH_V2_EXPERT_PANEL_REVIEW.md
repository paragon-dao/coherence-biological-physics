## THE BOTTOM LINE
================================================================================
There is a real, publishable paper hiding inside the empirical volume - but it is NOT the
paper as written. Four independent reviewers (neuro, CI, physics, skeptic) converged, without
coordination, on the SAME conclusion:

  Your genuine contribution is ONE idea: that the Kuramoto order parameter r is NOT collective
  intelligence, and that CI is UNIMODAL in r because it needs both integration and preserved
  independence - with the sharp, testable corollary that the optimal coupling K* RISES with
  viewpoint diversity gamma ("diverse groups need a richer channel to cohere"; K_c = 2 gamma).

Everything else - the inverted-U itself, the Kuramoto machinery, the bandwidth predictions,
BAGLE - the field already owns, and the paper currently CLAIMS what it should CITE. The single
most damaging flaw, named by all four: CI(r) proportional to r(1-r) is ASSERTED by hand and
then called a "theorem." It is not derived from the dynamics. Fix that one seam - derive the
optimum instead of positing it - and the paper turns from "disciplined poetry with a coupling
constant" (physicist) into a contribution.

The highest-value single move: produce ONE real inverted-U result (even a re-analysis of an
open dataset such as Dikker's) with a pre-registered, a-priori-specified optimum, in a metric
anyone can reproduce. One real curve with error bars beats forty pages of formalism.

## THE SHARPEST FIX (do this and the empirical paper becomes real)
================================================================================
Turn the ansatz into a derivation and extract a non-generic prediction:
1. DERIVE CI, don't posit it. Use Ott-Antonsen (2008) exact low-D dynamics for a Lorentzian g to
   define an information/decoding functional on the phase distribution and SHOW it is non-monotone
   in K. If you can locate the CI-maximizing coupling K* as a function of gamma FROM the dynamics,
   you have a theorem instead of r(1-r) by hand. (physicist + CI)
2. STATE THE KURAMOTO-SPECIFIC PREDICTION the paper is leaving on the table: near onset,
   r ~ sqrt(K - K_c) (supercritical pitchfork), plus its finite-size rounding. This is a signature
   no generic "monotone coupling" model shares - it is how you prove you are doing dynamics, not
   analogy. (physicist)
3. PUT TOPOLOGY IN. Replace mean-field with a network Kuramoto; K_c scales with 1/lambda_max.
   This makes 2.4 consistent with the math and connects to your directionality claims and to the
   CI-network literature (Centola, Becker, Almaatouq). (physicist + CI)
4. ENGAGE THE PARTIAL-SYNCHRONY / METASTABILITY / CRITICALITY literature - your inverted-U is
   THEIR result, developed rigorously in your own domain: chimera states (Abrams-Strogatz 2004;
   Panaggio-Abrams 2015), metastable coordination dynamics (Tognoli-Kelso 2014; Kelso 1995),
   criticality/edge-of-chaos (Chialvo 2010; Beggs-Plenz), Shanahan 2010 (metastable cognition in
   modular oscillator networks - almost a direct precedent). Say whether you subsume or rediscover.
