## PART 1 -- THE PITCH (documented, as delivered to "the chair")
===================================================================
I am not going to tell you I have found telepathy. The mystical version is dead,
and I retired it myself on a power-budget argument. What I want to show you is
smaller than telepathy, and I think it is bigger.

The claim, and it is falsifiable, which is why I am in your office and not on a
podcast: human beings are coupled oscillators. The heart drives a real,
measurable mechanical wave through the body -- reproducible beat to beat, and it
RINGS, like a struck bell, with a quality factor you expect from a resonator, not
from noise. That is one body. Now put the sensors on many bodies, in the wild,
and ask whether the rhythms find each other -- whether people drift into
coherence above chance, whether it rises with closeness and collapses under
stress, sickness, and isolation.

If that is true -- and it is a real IF, that is the whole point -- it proves that
connection is not a metaphor. It is physics. That "I feel you" and "we are in
sync" and "she lifted the whole room" are measurable coupling between
physiological oscillators. Every wisdom tradition has said we are not separate,
and science has said prove it. This proves it, with error bars, reproducible in
another lab.

Why now, and not ten years ago: the sensors finally got cheap, and the
distribution finally exists -- millions already carry the instruments in their
pockets. The measurement that was impossible is suddenly a Tuesday.

I do not know if it survives distance. That is the honest, thrilling crux. If it
is near-field, we have still proven collective coherence is real and buildable --
a field, a career. If it somehow survives distance with no channel, we have found
something that does not fit the physics we have. Either answer is publishable.
Either answer, you want to be in the room. I am asking to build the answer here,
with you.

## PART 2 -- THE STAKES (top possibilities: what a success could change)
===================================================================
Each is framed as: the mechanism, the intervention, and the honest boundary.

### A. LONELINESS, ISOLATION, AND SUICIDE -- from measured to interruptible
MECHANISM: loneliness is chronic decoherence -- an individual uncoupled from the
field. Crisis is very often preceded by MEASURABLE decoupling: physiological and
behavioral withdrawal, the rhythm going flat and disconnected before the person
can, or will, ask for help.
INTERVENTION: because coupling is measurable and the instruments are already on
people, the physiological signature of dangerous isolation could be detected IN
REAL TIME -- before the person reaches out, or when they cannot. Not surveillance:
care. Route gentle human connection, a crisis line, a call from someone who loves
them, at the moment the signal turns, not in the autopsy. (Haven already does
crisis detection today -- 100% crisis recall on its benchmark, 988 routing,
content-free safety. This is that, powered by the coherence signal, and aimed
upstream of the crisis.)
BOUNDARY: detection is a probability, not a verdict; consent and human-in-the-loop
are non-negotiable; the failure mode (false alarms, coercion, surveillance creep)
must be designed against from line one. The promise is help offered, never
control imposed.

### B. WAR, CONFLICT, AND OTHERING -- decoherence as the measurable substrate
MECHANISM: you cannot easily harm someone you are still resonating with.
Dehumanization is, at bottom, decoupling -- the refusal or inability to couple
with the other. Escalation toward violence is preceded by the physiological state
of disconnection. If coherence across group lines is measurable, so is its
collapse.
INTERVENTION: reconciliation and negotiation could be MEASURED for real coupling
rather than performed for cameras -- you would know when a peace process is
actually building coherence between former enemies and when it is theater. Design
rooms, rhythms, and contact that rebuild coupling across the line. Address the
physiological root of othering, not only its politics.
BOUNDARY: war has many causes -- resource, ideology, power -- that no biosignal
addresses. Coherence is one measurable dimension added to a hard problem, not a
peace machine. Aspirational, stated as such.

### C. THE ETHICAL INVERSION -- real-time signal processing, redeemed
THE POINT PHIL IS MAKING: we have become extraordinarily good at real-time signal
processing. Today that power is spent maximizing engagement -- the infinite
scroll, the wall, the thread -- extracting attention, often leaving people MORE
decohered than it found them. It is world-class capability aimed at low, even
negative, human value.
THE INVERSION: point that exact same real-time capability at the human COHERENCE
signal instead of the ad signal. Detect distress, decoupling, and crisis as they
happen. Intervene while it is happening, not after. The same microsecond-class
reflex philosophy that catches a hazard before a car hits it -- catch the
dangerous human state in the moment before harm. We already built the reflexes.
We have been pointing them at the wrong target.

### D. (adjacent, briefly) HEALING, TEAMS, GRIEF, LEARNING
If belonging is an engineering target: hospital wards, classrooms, marriages,
grieving families, high-stakes teams -- all become measurable for coherence and
designable for it. Not to control people; to help them find each other.

## PART 3 -- THE THROUGH-LINE (why this is ONE life's work, not two)
===================================================================
The reflex is the same primitive everywhere: catch the dangerous state in real
time, in the moment before it becomes harm. In a car, that is a collision
avoided. In a person, that is a crisis interrupted. In a group, that is a
fracture healed before it breaks. Fast reflex under slow reasoning -- in the
vehicle, in the AI, and in us. The AV safety work and the CMH work are not two
careers. They are the same instinct, aimed first at machines because machines
paid for the instruments, and ultimately at people, which was always the point.
